Chilli Potato Recipe

This restaurant-style crispy chilli potato recipe delivers perfectly crunchy potato wedges tossed in a sweet, spicy, and tangy Indo-Chinese sauce.

Ingredients:

For the Potatoes:

  • Potatoes: 3 large (peeled and cut into thick finger-length wedges)
  • Cornflour: 3 tbsp
  • Rice flour / Maida: 2 tbsp
  • Kashmiri red chilli powder: ½ tsp
  • Salt: To taste
  • Oil: For deep frying

For the Sauce & Toss:

  • Oil: 1½ tbsp
  • Garlic: 1 tbsp (finely chopped)
  • Ginger: 1 tsp (finely chopped)
  • Green chillies: 2 (slit lengthwise)
  • Onion: 1 medium (petals separated or cubed)
  • Capsicum (Bell pepper): 1 small (cubed)
  • Soy sauce: 1 tbsp
  • Green or Red chilli sauce: 1 tbsp
  • Tomato ketchup: 2 tbsp
  • Vinegar: 1 tsp
  • Cornflour slurry: 1 tsp cornflour dissolved in 2 tbsp water
  • Spring onion greens: 2 tbsp (chopped, for garnish)
  • White sesame seeds: 1 tsp (toasted, optional)

Method:

  1. Prep and Parboil the Potatoes
    1. Boil water in a large pot with ½ tsp salt.
    2. Add the potato wedges and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until they are slightly tender but still firm (parboiled).
    3. Drain the water completely and pat the potatoes dry on a kitchen towel
  2. Coat and Fry
    1. Transfer the dried potatoes to a mixing bowl.
    2. Sprinkle the cornflour, rice flour (or maida), red chilli powder, and a pinch of salt over them. Toss well until every potato wedge is evenly coated. Tip: Sprinkle 1 tsp of water if the flour is too dry to stick.
    3. Heat oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat.
    4. Fry the potatoes in batches until they turn crisp and golden brown. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Make the Chilli Sauce
    1. Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a wide pan or wok over high heat.
    2. Add the chopped ginger, garlic, and green chillies. Sauté for 30 seconds.
    3. Add the onion petals and capsicum cubes. Stir-fry on high heat for 1 to 2 minutes so they remain crunchy.
    4. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and vinegar.
    5. Pour in the cornflour slurry and stir continuously. Let the sauce simmer for 30 seconds until it glosses over and thickens
  4. Toss and Serve
    1. Turn off the heat (or keep it on ultra-low) and add the fried crispy potatoes to the sauce.
    2. Quickly toss everything together so the sauce coats the potatoes uniformly without making them soggy.
    3. Garnish with chopped spring onion greens and toasted sesame seeds. Serve immediately.
