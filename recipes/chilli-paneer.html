Chilli Paneer Recipe

Chilli Paneer is a popular Indo-Chinese appetizer featuring crispy batter-fried paneer cubes tossed in a sweet, spicy, and tangy sauce with bell peppers and onions. You can make it dry, semi-dry, or with a grav

Ingredients:

For the Paneer Batter & Frying

  • Paneer: 200g-250g cubed, with cornstarch, maida, black pepper, Kashmiri chili powder, salt, water, and oil for frying.

For the Sauce & Stir-Fry

  • Aromatics & Veggies: Oil, chopped garlic and ginger, green chilies, quartered onions, cubed capsicum, and spring onions.
  • Sauces & Seasonings: Soy sauce, red chili sauce, ketchup, vinegar, sugar, salt, pepper, and a cornstarch slurry.

Method:

  1. Coat and Fry the Paneer: Mix the batter ingredients with water to a thick consistency. Coat the paneer cubes and shallow or deep-fry until crisp and light golden, then drain.
  2. Sauté Veggies: Flash-sauté the ginger, garlic, chilies, onions, and capsicum on high heat for a minute or two to maintain a crunchy texture.
  3. Make the Sauce: Stir in the sauces, vinegar, seasonings, and the cornstarch slurry, cooking until the mixture thickens into a glossy glaze.
  4. Toss and Serve: Add the fried paneer to the sauce, toss on high heat, garnish with spring onion greens, and serve hot.
