Paneer Momos Recipe

This paneer momos recipe features soft, thin steamed flour wrappers stuffed with a savory, spiced paneer and crunchy vegetable filling. It delivers a perfect balance of a milky, crumbly texture enhanced by the sharp flavors of fresh garlic, ginger, and green chilies.

Ingredients:

For the Dough

  • All-purpose flour (maida): 1½ cups
  • Salt: ¼ teaspoon
  • Oil: 3 teaspoons
  • Water: As needed to knead a firm, smooth dough.

For the Paneer Filling

  • Grated paneer: 1.5 cups (approx. 200-250g)
  • Finely chopped garlic: 1 tablespoon
  • Finely chopped ginger: 1 tablespoon
  • Green chilies: 1-2 finely chopped
  • Finely chopped onion: 1 medium
  • Finely chopped veggies (cabbage, carrots, capsicum): ¼ cup each
  • Black pepper powder: ½ teaspoon
  • Salt: To taste
  • Oil or butter: 1-2 tablespoons for sautéing
  • Spring onion (whites and greens): Chopped

Method:-

  1. Make the Dough
    1. In a large bowl, mix the all-purpose flour and salt.
    2. Add the oil and gradually pour in water, kneading for about 5 minutes to form a firm, non-sticky dough.
    3. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 20 to 30 minutes.
  2. Prepare the Filling
    1. Heat a little oil or butter in a pan over medium heat.
    2. Add the chopped garlic, ginger, and green chilies, sautéing for a few seconds until fragrant.
    3. Add the chopped onions and mixed vegetables (cabbage, carrots, capsicum), and sauté on high heat for just 2 minutes so they stay slightly crisp.
    4. Turn off the heat and add the grated paneer, black pepper powder, spring onions, and salt. Mix well and let it cool. (Tip: Do not let the filling sit too long with salt, as onions release moisture.)
  3. Assemble and Shape the Momos
    1. Knead the rested dough for a few seconds and divide it into small, equal-sized balls.
    2. Dust a ball with flour and roll it out into a very thin circular disc (keep the edges slightly thinner than the center).
    3. Place 1 to 1.5 tablespoons of the paneer filling in the center.
    4. Pleat the edges together in a circle or half-moon shape, then pinch and twist the top to seal completely.
  4. Steam the Momos
    1. Bring water to a boil in a steamer or idli pot. Grease the steamer basket or line it with parchment paper so the momos don't stick.
    2. Arrange the momos with a little space between them.
    3. Cover and steam on medium-high heat for 10 to 15 minutes until the wrappers turn glossy and translucent.
