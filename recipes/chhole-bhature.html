Chhole Bhature Recipe

Chole Bhature is a classic Punjabi dish combining a spicy, dark chickpea curry with puffed, golden-fried leavened bread.

Ingredients:

  • Chickpeas (Kabuli Chana): 1 cup (soaked overnight with 1/4 tsp baking soda)
  • Whole Spices: 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 2 cloves, 2 green cardamoms, 1 black cardamom
  • Color Base: 1 tea bag or 1/2 tsp tea leaves boiled in water (for that classic dark brown color)
  • Masala Base: 1 finely chopped onion, 2 pureed tomatoes, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 green chilies
  • Spices: 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp chole masala, 1/4 tsp amchur (dry mango powder), and salt to taste

Method:

  1. Boil the Chana: Pressure cook the soaked chickpeas with the whole spices, tea water (strained), a pinch of salt, and 1/4 tsp baking soda for 4 to 5 whistles until soft and tender.
  2. Make the Masala: Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add cumin and whole spices, then sauté the onion paste until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, followed by the tomato puree.
  3. Add Powders: Mix in turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and chole masala. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Simmer: Add the boiled chickpeas along with the leftover cooking water. Let it simmer on low heat for 12 to 15 minutes so the flavors blend. Finish with amchur powder and fresh coriander leaves.
